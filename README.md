# Attention Is All You Need — Research Journey

This repository contains a student presentation and educational exploration of the original 2017 Transformer paper, *Attention Is All You Need*. It was prepared for the Research Journey course at HIT — Holon Institute of Technology.

## Contributors

- Shlomi Hazan
- Almog Schwartzberg
- Amit Herner

## Topics explored

- Limitations of RNNs, LSTMs, GRUs, and CNNs for sequence modeling
- The motivation for the Transformer architecture
- Query, Key, and Value representations
- Self-attention and multi-head attention
- Encoder and decoder components
- The original training setup and evaluation results
- Computational complexity and parallelization

The presentation is designed to make complex concepts accessible to beginners. It is a student presentation and review of published research, not original Transformer research or a claim of authorship of the paper.
